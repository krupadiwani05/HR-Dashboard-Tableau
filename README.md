# HR Analysis Dashboard | Tableau

> Understanding employee attrition through data-driven insights.

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze workforce data, explore employee attrition, and identify patterns across departments, gender, education, age groups, and job satisfaction.

## Dashboard Analysis

- **Attrition by Gender** — Compare employee attrition across genders.
- **Department-wise Attrition** — Identify departments experiencing employee turnover.
- **Education Field Analysis** — Explore attrition across educational backgrounds.
- **Age Group Analysis** — Examine employee distribution and attrition by age.
- **Job Satisfaction** — Analyze employee satisfaction ratings.
- **HR KPIs** — Track attrition rate, attrition count, and active employees.

## Tools & Techniques

**Tableau** · **Data Analysis** · **Data Visualization** · **Calculated Fields** · **Parameters**

## Objective

Transform HR data into meaningful insights to understand employee turnover, evaluate workforce trends, and support better HR decision-making.
